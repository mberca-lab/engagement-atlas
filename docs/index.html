07

Better questions.
Not easy verdicts.

Responsible people analytics makes the limits visible. The purpose of this atlas is to guide evidence-seeking conversations—not to automate judgments about workforces.

I / UNIT OF ANALYSIS

Organizations are not people.

These are public organization-level aggregates. They cannot explain an individual employee’s experience, identify subgroups, or support individual-level predictions. Avoid the ecological fallacy.

II / INFERENCE

A pattern is not a lever.

Correlation, clustering, and predictive importance are descriptive or predictive. None identifies the effect of an HR intervention. No causal uplift, business impact, or “what-if” promises are made here.

III / DECISION USE

Context before action.

Check survey definitions, response coverage, uncertainty, and local context. Pair the findings with employee listening and domain expertise before designing or evaluating a response.

Measurement and comparability

OPM FEVS engagement (EEI) is a percent-positive index on a 0–100 scale, composed of Leaders lead, Supervisory support, and Intrinsic work experience. Global satisfaction and the other survey percentages also use 0–100. A difference in index points is not a relative percentage change. Intent to leave is self-reported, not an actual exit rate.

Published respondent counts and response rates provide context but do not eliminate nonresponse bias. Changes in agency boundaries, instruments, wording, and response composition may compromise comparisons across years. Agency means should not be interpreted as employee-weighted federal estimates. See OPM documentation for exact item definitions and survey methodology.

What this analysis cannot establish

Aggregate data cannot diagnose individuals, demonstrate subgroup fairness, recover within-agency distributions, or identify causal intervention effects. Forecast evaluation in historical years does not guarantee transportability to later years, other sectors, or changing survey instruments.

Analysis design and reproducibility

Use the linked repository for source ingestion, cleaning decisions, feature definitions, temporal evaluation, and reproducible code. Source publications, not this visualization, remain authoritative.

Public-data boundary

Only fully public agency-level survey aggregates belong in this portfolio. No confidential telemetry, respondent-level records, de-anonymized customer lists, or synthetic substitute survey data are used. Public availability does not remove the obligation to respect source licensing and avoid stigmatizing interpretations.
